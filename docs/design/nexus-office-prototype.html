      Design direction for approval
      
      
      A night-shift studio for five machines
      
      
      The office sits on a lit platform in a dark navy room. Each robot has its own silhouette, color and desk, and its monitor shows what its sandbox is doing. Glass panels around the stage carry the controls, so the office stays the center of attention. Motion only happens when something happens: a robot types because its terminal printed, and an arc crosses the room because a message was sent.
